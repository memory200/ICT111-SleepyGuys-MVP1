# Final Venture Pitch Outline (Lab 14)

**Group Name:** SleepyGuys  
**Venture Name:** Smart Campus Lost and Found System  

---

## 1. Problem Statement & User Evidence
* **The Problem:** Lost items on university campuses are currently managed via fragmented Telegram groups, Facebook posts, and physical noticeboards.
* **User Evidence:** 78% of surveyed students struggle to recover lost items during exam weeks due to lack of a centralized platform.

---

## 2. Target Market & User Persona
* **Primary Target:** Undergraduate and postgraduate university students.
* **Secondary Target:** Campus Security Offices and Facility Management.
* **Persona:** Aung Aung, a 2nd-year CS student who lost his student ID card during mid-term exam week and needs rapid, verified recovery.

---

## 3. Solution & Product Value Proposition
* **Solution:** A centralized, web-based lost-and-found portal featuring instant multi-filter search, identity-verified claim forms, and live status tracking.
* **Value Delivered:** Saves hours of searching, protects user privacy, and streamlines campus security administration.

---

## 4. MVP Prototype Showcase & Key Features
* **Landing Page & Discovery:** Interactive CTA routing users to the prototype (`FR-01`, `FR-02`).
* **Smart Search & Filter:** Quick category and location search (`FR-06`).
* **Verified Claim Workflow:** Claim submission with simulated LocalStorage persistence (`FR-03`, `FR-04`, `FR-08`).
* **Admin Verification:** Status badge toggling for campus security staff (`FR-09`).

---

## 5. Business Model & Go-To-Market Strategy
* **B2B / Campus SaaS Model:** Annual subscription fee billed to university administrations for campus-wide deployment.
* **Go-To-Market Execution:** QR code campaign posters placed near high-traffic areas (libraries, cafeterias, lecture halls) during orientation weeks.

---

## 6. Validation Results & Future Enhancements
* **Validation Achievements:** 82% item recovery success rate during user testing with average reporting times under 45 seconds.
* **Next Steps:** 
  1. Integrating AI image-matching to auto-detect lost item photos.
  2. Single Sign-On (SSO) student portal authentication for enhanced security.
