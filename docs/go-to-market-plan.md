# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name
Smart Campus Lost and Found System

## Target Early Users
Active campus students who frequently attend lectures in major faculty buildings and student union members involved in lost item management.

## Main Value Proposition
Eliminate lost item confusion on campus by providing a centralized, real-time tracking portal.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class chat group | Students | Save time when items are lost | Click demo link | Clicks / Demo views | Students actively check chat groups daily |
| Campus poster QR | Students on campus | Report/search items quickly | Scan QR code | QR scans / Form visits | Placed directly near high-traffic loss zones (Cafeteria/Library) |
| Instagram story | Student community | Simple campus utility demo | Visit landing page | Story link clicks | High visual engagement for young student demographics |

## Launch Sequence
1. Prepare landing page and demo link (`/landing-page/index.html`).
2. Share promotional posts and QR codes across selected student channels.
3. Track early user engagement using `/data/acquisition-metrics-template.csv` and prototype logs.
4. Collect qualitative user feedback via the prototype demo screen.
5. Revise messaging, call-to-action text, or prototype UI based on user interaction insights.

## Risk and Mitigation
* **Risk:** Low user engagement due to chat message overload or lack of trust.
* **Mitigation:** Partner with Student Union representatives to share official posts and place physical QR posters in high-visibility campus zones like the library and cafeteria entrance.
