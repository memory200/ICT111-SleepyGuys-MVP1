## 1. Target User Persona & Situation
* **User:** Aung Aung, a 2nd-year CS student at RSU.

## 2. Value Proposition & Pitch Moment
* **Sales Pitch:** "Instead of scrolling through hundreds of unorganized chat messages, use the SleepyGuys Smart Campus Lost & Found portal to instantly search by category, check verified item listings, and claim your belongings securely."
* **Value Delivered:** Time saved, central accessibility, real-time status tracking, and identity-protected verification.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Interactive Demo Scenario Flow

1. **Discovery & Access:**
   * Aung Aung accesses the portal via the digital marketing link (`landing-page/index.html`).
   * He clicks on **"Try Interactive Demo"**, which routes him directly to the prototype (`prototype/index.html`).
   * *(Traceability: `REQ-NAV-01`)*

2. **Search & Filter:**
   * On the prototype dashboard, Aung Aung filters the category by "Electronics / Cards" and sets the location to "Central Library".
   * He quickly scans the search results grid.
   * *(Traceability: `REQ-SRC-01`)*

3. **Item Details & Claim Submission:**
   * He finds a matching record titled "Found Student ID & Laptop Charger - Library 1st Floor".
   * He clicks "View Details" to open the detail modal and verify the timestamp and item image.
   * He clicks "Claim Item" and fills out his contact information and unique details to prove ownership.
   * *(Traceability: `REQ-DET-01`, `REQ-CLM-01`)*

4. **Status Verification:**
   * The system logs the claim status in local storage, updating the item state to "Claim Pending".
   * Aung Aung receives a confirmation feedback message on screen.
   * *(Traceability: `REQ-ADM-01`)*
