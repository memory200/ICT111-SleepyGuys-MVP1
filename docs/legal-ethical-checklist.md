# Legal and Ethical Checklist

## 1. Legal Compliance
- **Data Protection:** The system only collects minimal operational data needed for item reporting and claim verification.
- **Copyright & IP:** All icons, UI kits, and fonts are used under open-source licenses (MIT/CC).

## 2. Ethical Considerations
- **Non-Discrimination:** All campus users (Students, Staff, Faculty) have equal access to report and search lost items.
- **Truthful Representation:** Item statuses (`Reported`, `Claimed`) accurately reflect physical security desk records without misleading users.
